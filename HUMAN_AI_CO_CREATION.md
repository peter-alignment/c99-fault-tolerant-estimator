## 3. Dynamic Quality Control & De-biasing Mechanisms

The validity of the YQ-HAET framework relies on active calibration rather than passive reliance. During the synthesis of the 16 monographs, several critical failure modes inherent to autoregressive models were systematically countered:

1. **Suppression of Statistical Hallucinations**:
   - *Risk*: Standard generative models default to unconstrained averaging when simulating contact dynamics, producing non-physical penetration or kinetic energy explosion.
   - *Intervention*: The human researcher imposed strict non-smooth contact mechanics and Lyapunov kinetic energy bounding, forcing the synthetic system to operate within non-linear manifold constraints.

2. **Deterministic Embedded Constraints**:
   - *Risk*: AI code generators commonly introduce dynamic allocation (`malloc`/`free`) or unbounded recursion.
   - *Intervention*: Human-mandated zero-heap policies and strict execution bounds (WCET $\le 11.82\ \mu\text{s}$ on ARM Cortex-M7), rejecting all non-deterministic constructs.

3. **Metadata and Citation Topology Auditing**:
   - *Risk*: Automated metadata generation frequently introduces citation drift, misattributed DOIs, or schema validation failures under DataCite standards.
   - *Intervention*: Multi-pass validation of the 16-part sequential DOI schema, guaranteeing exact bidirectionality between Zenodo deposition records and the GitHub kernel release.
